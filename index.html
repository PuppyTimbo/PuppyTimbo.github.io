Puppy Timbo (Instagram: @puppy_timbo) is a German content creator from the queer pup play community. Active as a pup since 2016, he stands for an urban alpha style with streetwear, sneakers and MX gear and is known for his neon-green mask. On Instagram and TikTok he posts bilingual (German/English) photos, reels, the series #GasmaskMonday and the educational series Pup Play 101. Behind the mask he is a trained IT specialist. His motto: “Diversity makes us strong.”
